# FormulaPad User Guide

Welcome to **FormulaPad**, an interactive visual formula calculator and physics simulation environment designed for students, educators, and engineers.

---

## 1. Navigating Formulas
- **Search & Filters**: Use the global search bar to instantly query by formula title, equation symbol, or STEM domain (Physics, Mathematics, Chemistry, Engineering).
- **Category Tabs**: Switch between dedicated sub-disciplines (Kinematics, Thermodynamics, Optics, Calculus, Fluid Dynamics, etc.).
- **Favorites & History**: Star frequently used formulas for instant one-click access in your favorites shelf.

---

## 2. Interactive Simulations & Calculators
- **Parameter Sliders**: Drag live sliders or type exact numbers into numeric input boxes.
- **Environment Presets**: Click quick celestial buttons (Earth, Moon, Mars, Jupiter) to test how physics variables behave under different planetary conditions.
- **Visual Models**: Observe real-time SVG updates (such as harmonic pendulum oscillations, wave interference, projectile trajectories, or circuit loops).

---

## 3. Step-by-Step Derivations
- Expand the **Substitution & Steps** accordion under any active formula to view the full algebraic formula breakdown, variable substitutions, intermediate values, and final units.

---

## 4. Pan & Zoom Workspace Controls
- **Zoom Slider**: Adjust the canvas zoom between 50% and 200%.
- **Reset**: Double-tap or click **Reset Zoom** to return to standard 100% scale.
- **2D Pan**: Freely pan horizontally and vertically on touch screens and trackpads without negative coordinate clipping.
